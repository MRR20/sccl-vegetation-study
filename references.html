How the literature shaped the study

Trend methods (Mann–Kendall and Sen's slope on annual Landsat NDVI) follow Ranjan et al. (2022), Han et al. (2021), Dadhich et al. (2026) and Moon et al. (2026). Testing climate alongside mining follows Li et al. (2026) and Liu et al. (2026); Liu et al. also flag the autocorrelation problem in short NDVI series. Garai and Narayana (2018) and Bawankar et al. (2026) are the closest earlier studies of the Godavari coalfield and SCCL mines. Karan et al. (2016), Saini (2024), Hu et al. (2022) and Wang et al. (2026) cover reclamation monitoring, and Xiao et al. (2020) the LandTrendr approach planned for mine-level disturbance mapping.
