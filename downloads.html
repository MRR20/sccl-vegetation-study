Data tables

All tables are CSV with a header row. NDVI is unitless, rainfall is in mm, temperature in °C and production in lakh tonnes.

  • NDVI zone means, 2005–20261 KBMean January–February NDVI for the 0–1, 1–3 and 3–5 km zones and the whole 5 km area.
  • Mann–Kendall and Sen's slope results0 KBS, τ, Z, p, Sen's slope, lag-1 autocorrelation and pre-whitened p for each zone.
  • Correlations: raw, detrended and first differences3 KBPearson r and p for every NDVI zone and driver, in all three forms.
  • Matched NDVI, climate and production table4 KBThe 21 aligned years used for the correlation and regression analysis (2004–05 total corrected).
  • Rainfall and temperature, 2004–20252 KBIMD annual and monsoon rainfall and ERA5 annual and January–February temperature, with anomalies.
  • SCCL coal production1 KBOpencast, underground and total production in lakh tonnes by financial year.

The raw Landsat scenes are not included because of their size (several GB). They can be downloaded from USGS EarthExplorer using the scene dates in Table 3.2.
